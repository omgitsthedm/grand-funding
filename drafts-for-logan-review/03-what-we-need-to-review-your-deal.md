# What we need to review your deal

*Draft for Logan's review. Business-purpose loans for real estate investors only. Not for owner-occupied or consumer purposes.*

The fastest way to get a clear answer is to send a complete picture up front. Here's what Logan looks at.

## The property

- Address (Arizona or California)
- Property type and current condition
- Photos or an inspection report, if you have them
- Purchase contract, or current value if you already own it

## The money

- Purchase price
- Funds you're requesting
- Any existing loans on the property, with current balances
- Your renovation or construction budget, itemized if possible

## The plan

- What you're going to do with the property
- Your after-repair value and the recent sales that support it
- How you'll repay the loan: sale, refinance, or another source
- What happens if the sale or refinance takes longer than planned

## You

- The entity you'll borrow through (for example, an LLC) and its documents
- A short summary of your experience: deals completed and roles held
- Any contract dates or deadlines we need to know about

## What happens next

Logan reviews the request personally and follows up with written terms or with questions. Credit, income, experience, and documentation requirements are determined after review of the specific transaction.

Rates, fees, loan sizing, leverage, term length, and timing are provided only after that review. This is not a commitment to lend.
